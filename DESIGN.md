## Brand & Style

This design system expresses heavy-duty structural reliability, masterful woodworking, and European construction standards tailored for residential and commercial clients across Pazardzhik and Plovdiv. 

### Visual Character
- **Archetype:** The Master Builder & Structural Conservator. Authentic, enduring, safety-focused, and unpretentious.
- **Design Movement:** Industrial Tactile Minimalism. It pairs precision architectural geometry with warm, physical material cues (charcoal slate, planed oak, safety signaling).
- **Target Audience:** Homeowners renovating heritage roofs, commercial facility managers requiring structural timber repair, and architectural developers in South-Central Bulgaria seeking licensed, durable craftsmanship.
- **Emotional Response:** Solidity, total structural integrity, localized competence, and clear technical transparency without corporate bloat.

## Colors

The palette directly models real job-site materials and industrial safety protocols:

- **Primary (`#1E293B` Slate Charcoal):** Imparts dense weight, roof tile slate tone, and structural permanence. Used for primary typography, dominant structural containers, and grounding elements.
- **Secondary (`#B45309` Seasoned Timber):** Represents warm Bulgarian oak, pine beams, and cured wood preservation. Used for master craft badges, wood service categories, and secondary accents.
- **Tertiary (`#EA580C` High-Visibility Safety Amber):** Derived from safety wear, site scaffolding, and immediate technical action. Reserved exclusively for direct contact points, 24/7 emergency dispatch banners, quick call triggers, and urgent quote actions.
- **Neutral (`#F8FAFC` Lime-Wash Chalk):** Crisp architectural background tone providing optimal readability against rich slate and amber elements.

### Functional Roles
- Backgrounds prioritize clean, high-albedo whites (`#FFFFFF`) and architectural off-white (`#F8FAFC`) to evoke cleanliness and modern project delivery.
- Slate charcoal is applied at high contrast against chalk surfaces to guarantee outdoor smartphone legibility under direct sunlight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Outfit** for structural headings with **Inter** for dense technical communication and Cyrillic/Bulgarian script legibility.

- **Headings (Outfit):** Geometric, solid, and decisive. Bold and Extra Bold weights provide industrial impact reminiscent of steel markers and site engineering blueprints.
- **Body & Technical Specs (Inter):** Highly legible at all sizes with neutral humanist properties that render Bulgarian Cyrillic characters clearly without decorative artifacts.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Standardized to medium/bold weights with slight tracking adjustments for equipment spec labels, permit certifications, and technical guarantees.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, modular grid forms the foundation of all layouts, reflecting architectural frameworks and construction blueprints:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid within a 1280px master frame. Outer margins set to `margin` (3rem), with `gutter` (1.5rem) column spacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 2rem outer margins and 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout utilizing `margin-mobile` (1.25rem) to ensure large tap targets and maximum horizontal scan space.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm strictly adheres to an 8px base unit system.
- Component padding uses `space-md` for standard forms/chips and `space-xl` for heavy structural cards and project comparison panels.
- Content layouts stack vertically on mobile to prioritize emergency call buttons and geographic verification modules (Pazardzhik / Plovdiv service zones).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and tactile low-contrast borders rather than deep, diffused shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC`
  - Elevated work planes / cards: `#FFFFFF`
  - Technical inset modules / footer anchors: `#1E293B`
- **Ghost Borders:** Flat structural framing with `1px solid rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)` provides a clean architectural contour on cards and technical tables.
- **Tactile Shadows:** Minimal and grounded. Interactive cards use subtle offsets: `0 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active State Depth:** Hover actions trigger an elevated border highlight in warm wood (`#B45309`) with an edge offset of `0 12px 24px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Soft (`1`)** roundedness scale, balancing the crisp, chiseled edges of cut timber and slate tile with subtle corner softness for usability.

- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 0.25rem (`rounded`). Delivers a precise, industrial finish without harshness.
- **Structural Containers (Cards, Modals, Banners):** 0.5rem (`rounded-lg`). Keeps large content areas grounded and organized.
- **Trust Badges & Chips:** 0.25rem corner radius; never circular or organic. Chamfer-like restraint reinforces architectural discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Emergency / Immediate Quote):** High-visibility safety orange (`#EA580C`) background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), Outfit medium/bold type, 0.25rem radius. Elevated subtly on hover with a darken transition (`#C2410C`).
- **Secondary Action (Timber Services / Portfolio):** Deep slate (`#1E293B`) solid fill with white text, or pure white with a 1.5px `#1E293B` stroke.
- **Quick Call Fixed Anchor:** Prominent mobile-pinned bar utilizing `#EA580C` with a phone icon, primary phone number in high-contrast white, and localized service radius label ("Pazardzhik • Plovdiv • 24/7").

### Cards & Service Units
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`), 0.5rem radius, and internal padding of `space-lg`.
- Timber restoration modules feature a top accent border (3px) in `#B45309`.
- Roofing service cards feature technical specifications: slope angle compatibility, guaranteed warranty durations (e.g., "15 Years"), and certified timber origin chips.

### Trust Badges & Guarantee Seals
- Structured rectangular badges displaying regional verification ("Лицензиран строител Пловдив / Пазарджик"), European material standard marks (EN codes), and safety adherence logos.
- Backgrounds use a light timber tint (`rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.08)`) with `#B45309` typography and borders.

### Input Fields & Estimation Calculators
- Form inputs feature white backgrounds, slate text (`#1E293B`), 1px neutral border (`#CBD5E1`), and `space-md` internal padding.
- Focused inputs shift to a 2px `#B45309` timber outline.
- Includes quick-select square chips for project scope: *New Roof*, *Timber Treatment*, *Emergency Leak Repair*, *Structural Restoration*.

### Lists & Scope Breakdowns
- Bullet points replaced with custom structural checkmark icons colored in `#B45309`.
- Multi-row repair stages presented with left-aligned slate rules (`2px solid #E2E8F0`) forming a chronological build-log aesthetic.
